# QiPAI Framework Roadmap

This document outlines the future development plans for the QiPAI framework, including planned features, improvements, and research directions.

## Current Status (v0.1.0)

The initial version of QiPAI provides a complete foundation for quantum-inspired computing and AI:

- ✅ Core quantum state and circuit simulation
- ✅ Quantum neural networks for hybrid classical-quantum models
- ✅ Phase-based autonomous reasoning agents
- ✅ Domain-specific quantum programming language
- ✅ Visualization tools including 3D state visualizations
- ✅ Integration with IBM Quantum hardware
- ✅ Comprehensive documentation and examples

## Research Directions

QiPAI's development is guided by research in several cutting-edge areas:

### 1. Quantum Information Theory

Exploring how quantum information concepts can enhance classical AI, even without quantum hardware.

### 2. Quantum Algorithm Design

Developing new algorithms that leverage quantum principles for computational advantage.

### 3. Quantum-Classical Interfaces

Investigating optimal ways to partition problems between quantum and classical components.

### 4. Phase-Based Computation

Researching how phase relationships can be exploited for novel computational models.

### 5. Quantum-Inspired Cognitive Architectures

Applying quantum principles to model human-like cognitive functions.

## Versioning and Release Schedule

QiPAI follows semantic versioning:

- **Patch releases** (e.g., 0.1.1): Bug fixes and minor improvements, released as needed
- **Minor releases** (e.g., 0.2.0): New features and enhancements, approximately every 2-3 months
- **Major releases** (e.g., 1.0.0): Significant milestones with potential breaking changes, approximately yearly

## Feedback and Prioritization

This roadmap is not fixed and will evolve based on community feedback and research developments. We encourage users to provide input on what features would be most valuable for their applications.
